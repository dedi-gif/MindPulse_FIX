# **App Name**: MindPulse

## Core Features:

- Daily Mood Check-in: Users log their daily mood by selecting from a range of emotions.
- AI Journaling Prompts: AI suggests journaling prompts to aid deeper reflection and exploration of feelings and emotions.
- Detailed Journaling: Users can write detailed journal entries to capture their thoughts, feelings, and experiences, enhancing their daily mood check-in.
- Mood History: Allows users to view their mood tracking history through interactive charts and calendars.
- Personalized Insights: Generative AI tool analyzes mood data to identify patterns and provide personalized insights and recommendations for improving mental well-being.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Soft lavender (#E6E6FA), evoking calmness and serenity.
- Background color: Pale purple (#F5F5FF), creating a gentle and soothing backdrop.
- Accent color: Light sky blue (#B0E2FF), providing a refreshing and uplifting contrast.
- Body and headline font: 'PT Sans', a humanist sans-serif providing a balance of modernity and warmth for easy reading.
- Use minimalistic and gentle icons representing different emotions and activities.
- Clean and intuitive layout with ample white space to reduce visual clutter and enhance user experience.
- Subtle animations and transitions to create a smooth and engaging user experience.
